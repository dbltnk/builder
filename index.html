H O W T O P L A Y
  1. Click a palette tile, then click an empty cell to place it.
  2. Drag across cells to paint a run of belts.
  3. Right-click a placed tile to rotate it. Click it for more options.
  4. Drag a placed tile onto the palette area to delete it.
  5. Hotkeys: 1…0 and Shift+1…0 select/place palette tiles. Tab/Shift+Tab cycles palette.
  6. Sim hotkeys (shown in buttons): Space play/pause, M step forward, N step backward, F rotate hovered tile, R/D delete hovered tile, Backspace/Delete clear board, Z undo, X redo, +/- speed.
  7. Press play to run the simulator. Match the target output to win.
